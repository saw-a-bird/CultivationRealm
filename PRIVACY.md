# Cultivation Realm – Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 7, 2026**

Cultivation Dev respects your privacy. This policy explains how Cultivation Realm handles information when you use the application.

### Information stored by the application

Cultivation Realm stores application data such as your tasks, projects, focus sessions, progress, and settings locally on your device.

This information is stored in your local application data folder. It is not transmitted to Cultivator Dev or any third party by the application.

Users may enter personal information into task or project descriptions. Any such information remains in the application's local data unless the user chooses to share it themselves.

### Data collection and sharing

Cultivation Realm does not use user accounts, advertising, analytics, or tracking services. It does not collect or transmit personal information to external servers.

### Data security and control

Your application data remains on your device. You can remove it by deleting the application's local data files. Please note that doing so will remove your saved progress and settings.

### Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the application's features or data practices change.
